MATH INVADERS 3D

Pick your squadron. The squadron chooses your operation —
the ships inside it are just for looks.
Flying: drag anywhere to look around — left and right to turn, up and down to pitch. Tap a fact out in the sector to swing straight onto it.

Tilt to Look turns the device into a window on the sector, the way a stargazing app works. Hold it up in front of you and turn on the spot: the view goes wherever the back of the device points, and comes back when you do. Whichever way you are facing when a flight begins counts as straight ahead. With tilt on, tap a fact to lock onto it rather than to swing the view.

Fighting: the fact you are pointed at locks as your target and its three answers appear in the panel. Tap one to fire it. A fact that reaches you halves your shields and is remembered; it comes back as a green asteroid, and answering that one right gives the shields back.
